# Code of Conduct

We want a friendly, inclusive community. By participating, you agree to:

## Our standards
- Be respectful and considerate.
- Assume good intent.
- Give constructive feedback.
- Respect different viewpoints and experiences.

## Unacceptable behavior
- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Threats, intimidation, or trolling.
- Sharing private information without consent.

## Enforcement
Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to keep the community safe.

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the project maintainers through the repository issues.
