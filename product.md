# Product: Student Payment & Expense Tracker

## 1. Product Overview

A mobile-first payment and expense tracker designed for students who manage small but frequent transactions (UPI, card, cash) and want a simple way to see where their money goes, stay within a monthly allowance, and save for short‑term goals like gadgets, trips, or exam fees.[web:19][web:22][web:33]  

The app focuses on three things: fast logging, light automation (where possible), and clear, non‑intimidating visuals that help students make quick day‑to‑day decisions rather than complex financial plans.[web:19][web:29][web:30]  

---

## 2. Target Users

- College and university students (typically 18–25) managing:
  - Fixed or semi‑fixed monthly allowances from parents.
  - Stipends from internships or part‑time work.
  - Shared living costs with roommates or hostel friends.[web:17][web:19]  
- Students who:
  - Use UPI and cards heavily for small, frequent payments.
  - Struggle to understand where their money went at the end of the month.
  - Are new to budgeting and want a lightweight, friendly tool.[web:19][web:33]  

---

## 3. Core Value Proposition

- Give students an instant, accurate picture of:
  - How much they have left to spend today/this week/this month.
  - What they are spending on the most.
  - Whether they are on track for key savings goals.[web:19][web:22][web:29]  
- Reduce mental load and friction:
  - Minimal manual typing.
  - Smart defaults and suggestions.
  - Clear alerts before overspending, not after.[web:29][web:30][web:36]  

---

## 4. Key Use Cases

1. Track daily spending against a monthly allowance.  
2. Avoid blowing money on food, rides, or impulse buys by seeing real‑time category totals.  
3. Split rent, utilities, or trips with roommates and settle up cleanly.  
4. Save toward a specific item (laptop, phone, exam fees, trip) with visible progress.  
5. Review last month to adjust budgets and spending habits.[web:19][web:22][web:33]  

---

## 5. Feature Groups

### 5.1 Account & Onboarding

- **Simple sign‑up and profile**
  - Create an account with minimal friction.
  - Set preferred currency and language.
  - Optionally indicate whether the user has a fixed monthly allowance or variable income.[web:29][web:32]  

- **Initial setup wizard**
  - Ask for approximate monthly allowance/income.
  - Suggest baseline budgets for common categories (Food, Transport, Rent, Subscriptions, Shopping, Others).
  - Let users adjust or skip to keep it lightweight.[web:19][web:22][web:33]  

---

### 5.2 Transactions & Categories

- **Add income and expenses**
  - Quick form to log amount, category, date, and payment method (UPI, card, cash, wallet).
  - Optional notes (e.g., “Birthday treat”, “Cab to college”).[web:19][web:29][web:36]  

- **Flexible categories**
  - Predefined student‑relevant categories.
  - Ability to create custom categories and emojis/icons for personalization.[web:19][web:29][web:33]  

- **Tags for context**
  - Optional tags like “Exam”, “Trip”, “Club”, “Side‑hustle” to group expenses across categories (e.g., total spent on a specific trip).[web:29][web:35]  

- **Search and filters**
  - Filter by date range, category, payment method, or tag.
  - Text search on notes and merchants.[web:29][web:37]  

---

### 5.3 Budgets & Allowance Management

- **Monthly and weekly budgets**
  - Global monthly budget tied to allowance/income.
  - Optional per‑category budgets (e.g., Food 3k, Transport 1k).[web:19][web:22][web:29]  

- **Daily allowance view**
  - Show “safe to spend today” based on remaining monthly budget and days left.
  - Adjust dynamically when spending is over or under plan.[web:17][web:22]  

- **Budget progress display**
  - Clear visualization of “spent vs remaining” for:
    - Total monthly budget.
    - Key categories.
    - Current week.[web:22][web:24][web:30]  

- **Overspend handling**
  - Indicate how much the user is over budget.
  - Suggest how much to cut in the remaining days to get back on track.[web:19][web:29]  

---

### 5.4 Analytics & Insights

- **Home dashboard**
  - This month’s total spent and remaining budget.
  - Top categories by spend.
  - Quick view of upcoming bills and goals progress.[web:19][web:24][web:29]  

- **Spending trends**
  - Charts for spending by day/week.
  - Month‑over‑month comparison (e.g., “You spent 15% more on food than last month”).[web:24][web:29][web:36]  

- **Category breakdown**
  - Pie or bar chart of categories for a selected period.
  - Tap into a category to see individual transactions.[web:19][web:24][web:30]  

- **Simple insights**
  - Lightweight, student‑friendly messages like:
    - “Food is your largest expense this month.”
    - “If you keep spending like this, you will exceed your budget in X days.”
    - “You have maintained your budget streak for 7 days.”[web:29][web:32][web:38]  

---

### 5.5 Goals & Savings

- **Goal setup**
  - Create goals such as “New phone 25k”, “Trip 10k”, “Emergency fund 5k”.
  - Set target date for each goal.[web:19][web:29][web:32]  

- **Goal contributions**
  - Mark transfers to a “goal” as separate from everyday spending.
  - Track total saved vs target and percentage completion.[web:19][web:29]  

- **Progress feedback**
  - Show how much needs to be saved per week/month to stay on track.
  - Notify when a goal is nearing completion.[web:29][web:32]  

---

### 5.6 Shared Expenses & Roommates

- **Shared expense creation**
  - Mark an expense as “shared”.
  - Add participants (friends/roommates) and split equally or by custom amounts.[web:20][web:22]  

- **Balances summary**
  - For each group or friend, show:
    - “You owe X.”
    - “They owe you Y.”[web:20][web:24]  

- **Settlement flow**
  - Provide a simple summary screen with all pending amounts.
  - Let users mark a balance as “settled” once payment is made via any external payment method.[web:20][web:24]  

---

### 5.7 Bills, Subscriptions & Reminders

- **Bill & subscription list**
  - List of recurring expenses: rent, Wi‑Fi, OTT, music, cloud, coaching, etc.
  - Amount, frequency, and due date for each.[web:19][web:24][web:32]  

- **Calendar and timeline view**
  - Monthly view of upcoming bills and major expenses.
  - Highlight “heavy” weeks where many payments fall together.[web:24][web:29]  

- **Smart reminders**
  - Alerts before due dates.
  - Early warning when upcoming bills plus current spend will exceed budget.[web:19][web:24][web:29]  

- **Subscription awareness**
  - Highlight under‑used or rarely used subscriptions (e.g., “You’ve paid for X for 3 months but opened it once”).[web:19][web:24][web:38]  

---

### 5.8 Automation & Capture (Conceptual)

> These are product‑level behaviors, not technical details.

- **Smart transaction suggestions**
  - When similar transactions repeat, suggest category or tag based on history.
  - Learn user preferences over time.[web:19][web:24][web:29]  

- **Receipt capture**
  - Allow users to attach a photo of a receipt to a transaction for later reference.
  - Optionally pre‑fill amount/date from the image if available.[web:24][web:29][web:36]  

- **Pattern detection**
  - Identify recurring payments that the user has not yet marked as “bills/subscriptions”.
  - Suggest adding them to the Bills section.[web:19][web:24][web:32]  

---

### 5.9 Engagement & Motivation

- **Streaks and achievements**
  - Streaks for:
    - Logging every day.
    - Staying within budget for a week/month.
    - Saving towards a goal every week.[web:19][web:22][web:33]  

- **Badges and milestones**
  - Unlockable badges like:
    - “First month under budget”.
    - “Goal completed”.
    - “Tracked expenses for 30 days straight”.[web:19][web:33]  

- **Personalized tips**
  - Occasional, concise tips based on actual behavior:
    - “You tend to overspend in the last week of the month.”
    - “Consider reducing food delivery by 10% to meet your savings goal.”[web:29][web:32][web:38]  

---

### 5.10 Privacy, Security & Trust (At Product Level)

- **Privacy controls**
  - Clear explanation of what financial information is stored and why.
  - Ability to hide sensitive amounts on screen (e.g., blur totals until tapped).[web:29][web:32]  

- **Local privacy features**
  - Optional app‑level lock screen (PIN/biometric).
  - Quick “screen privacy” mode for showing someone only a specific view (e.g., goal progress) without revealing all balances.[web:29][web:32]  

- **Data ownership & export**
  - Allow users to export their data in a simple format for backup or sharing with parents/mentors.[web:29][web:37]  

---

### 5.11 Settings & Personalization

- **Customization of home screen**
  - Let users choose which cards/widgets appear on the main dashboard:
    - Daily allowance.
    - Goals.
    - Upcoming bills.
    - Shared balances.[web:30][web:33]  

- **Localization**
  - Local currency, date format, and optional localized category presets.[web:29][web:32]  

---

## 6. Non‑Goals (For Clarity)

- No full‑service banking or lending (credit cards, loans, etc.) inside this product version.  
- No complex investing features (direct stocks, derivatives, etc.); focus remains on spending, basic savings, and short‑term student goals.[web:19][web:32]  

The product is intentionally scoped to be a clear, approachable companion for students’ everyday money decisions, not a heavy all‑in‑one finance platform.[web:19][web:22][web:33]
